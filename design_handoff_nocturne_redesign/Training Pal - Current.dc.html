Reading this back, the honest diagnosis: the app is structurally sound but visually undifferentiated — one green used for the accent, every action, every "current" highlight and every success state at once; three grey card fills doing similar jobs; numbers (weights, reps, timers) set in the same UI face and weight as the labels around them, so nothing that matters mid-set is bigger or calmer than anything else. Next turn I'll bring Nocturne in and explore directions on Today and the live workout screen first.
